New in AquaTrace 2.0: optional automatic sponsored shopping suggestions. Your AI-usage tracking works whether you enable these or not.

When on, AquaTrace sends AON the product title, category and price of the product page you view, or the search words you type into Amazon/Flipkart search. It never sends the page URL, cookies, account or seller data, page text, AI chats or local history.

Price includes its currency. If product context is unavailable on a supported shopping page, a cleaned search request or generic offer request is used. This is off by default; nothing is sent to AON until you tick the box. You can turn it off any time in the AquaTrace popup. Your previous popup-only choice is not carried over to this refreshed automatic feature.

Offers are labeled Sponsored and may earn AquaTrace a commission. As with any HTTPS connection, AON may see network metadata such as your IP address.
